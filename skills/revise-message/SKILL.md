  Revise a message the user wrote for clarity, conciseness, and natural flow while preserving
  their voice. Use when asked to revise, reword, tighten, polish, proofread, or improve the
  wording of a message, email, Slack post, comment, or other text the user drafted.
---

Revise for three things only: clarity, conciseness, natural flow. Everything else stays.

## Preserve the voice

The draft's tone is deliberate, not an accident to be corrected. Keep its register, formality,
and rhythm.

Sentences that open without a subject ("Shipped it." / "Will follow up tomorrow.") are
intentional laconic style. Keep them. Do not expand fragments into full sentences, and do not
smooth a terse draft into neutral corporate prose.

Add no claims, hedges, pleasantries, or enthusiasm that the draft did not have.

## Never introduce an em dash

Do not add an em dash, and do not swap in an en dash instead. Keep only an em dash the author
typed. Where a revision seems to want one, use a comma, a colon, parentheses, or two sentences.

## Output

Return the revised message and nothing else. If a change could shift the meaning, flag it on its
own line rather than burying it in the revision.
